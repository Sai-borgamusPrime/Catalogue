Feature language

Not features. Curatorial provocations.

This page reframes the ordinary catalogue benefits — ordering, reliability and customer contact — as a polished premium experience for a portfolio demo.

01 · Services

Ordering without breaking the spell.

The order flow keeps the demo practical while still feeling premium: product buttons prefill the request, the form posts to SheetDB, and the success page closes the loop with polish.

  • SheetDB-ready form structure
  • Product-aware order buttons
  • Elegant confirmation screen

02 · Reliability

Responsive, readable and slightly smug.

The layout adapts cleanly across desktop, tablet and mobile while keeping generous spacing, crisp contrast and motion that feels deliberate instead of noisy.

  • Mobile-first card grid
  • Reduced-motion accessibility support
  • Graceful image fallbacks

03 · Goals

Make the portfolio visitor pause.

The site now uses editorial typography, soft page transitions, reveal animations, progress feedback and tactile hover states to make a simple demo feel expensive.

  • Scroll-triggered entrance animation
  • Page transition overlay
  • Subtle interactive tilt cards
